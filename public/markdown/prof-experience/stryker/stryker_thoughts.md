<h2 class="master-center">Some personal thoughts on what I learned </h2>

While in hindsight this fact is obvious, but the amount of people testing a product is significantly greater
than the amount of people developing the device itself.
This can actually be seen in the military - there are so much more people in logistics than are frontline soldiers.
It made me appreciate software that actually follows the
engineering procedure.<br/><br/>

With the modern world running on the thought process of "write some junk to make it work
and push it, if it breaks we can always push an update", being forced to apply rigor to software verification
gave me appreciation to the testing that goes into safety critical devices.<br/><br/>

The people that I worked with are all very smart engineers. It was honestly a pleasure to shadow them and be 
treated as an equal there :). Shoutout to Elsayed, Bill, Vin, Casey, Ben, and Mark.
